# Beton Ikon 7x7

The artwork is based on a series of 49 concrete icons arranged in a 7x7 grid. Each has a dimension of 70cm x 70cm which builds up a structure scaling 4.9m x 4.9m and weighing over 1225 Kg.

![_full parallax(coeff:1) caption:'All the icons arranged in their grid'](beton_ikon_7x7/1.webp)

### PICTURE IN PICTURE, LAYER IN LAYER.

Each of the icons can also stand on their own, but together they create a new image. It’s a storyboard, a loop, a fractal, a self-biting snake in its own mouth - a cycle that is constantly rebooting.

These frames, taken out and placed side by side, form a new image, which is the motif of the three rabbits in the background. Each of these images and the rabbit itself is a paraphrase - famous paintings, sculptures, a series of movements. The artwork builds bridges across European art history, and the clothes on the figures are generated by artificial intelligence, based on keywords. Despite the same instructions, the results are different, but similar, centered around a mystical, magical, transcendent myth. The images only show female figures and rabbits. Self-portraits?

![_full parallax(coeff:1)](beton_ikon_7x7/2.webp)

One might wonder whether it is Olga in the pictures or just pictures of Olga, or are we seeing roles in created compositions? Where does reality end and where does simulation begin? Digital photos are manipulated and turned into concrete icons, and in doing so, they are destroyed. Mica, pigment, gold and crystals appear on their surfaces. They are compositions in themselves, and together they create something new. In their combination we can see the fourth dimension, making sense of a series of so-far meaningless events. In the background, the three rabbits, their ears together are also an optical game - each rabbit has two ears and they run in a circle.

The motif of the three rabbits has a special symbolism. Its origins date back to ancient times: it appears in stone carvings, clay pottery, buildings or sculptures, where the three interlock. The ears of three interconnected rabbits have many meanings in different cultures and religions. This motif is not only visually interesting, but also carries deeper philosophical and spiritual layers, which in my art I try to express in my art.

They are the artifacts of an imagined, lost personal creed. A private mythology as an origin story. Explanation to everything where 42 in this case is 49.
