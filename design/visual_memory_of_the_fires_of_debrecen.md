## Visual Memory of the Fires of Debrecen
**Debreceni tűzesetek vizuális emlékezete**

![_parallax(side) invert](visual_memory_of_the_fires_of_debrecen/wireframe.svg)

<span class="mdFade">

Permanent data physicalization sculpture  
Glass, wood, steel, concrete, plexi-glass, UV print  
1.4 m ↔ x 1.4 m ⤢ x 3 m ↥

</span>

Debrecen, a historic city proud of its past, unfortunately faces challenges from time to time, including fire incidents. However, these trials not only bring dark moments, but also inspiration for human creation. This installation, which shines in the form of a glass crystal, reminds us of just such events. Images of fires captured through data visualisation appear on the glass panels, evoking the tragedies of the past, but also the materials of reconstruction: wood, glass, metal and concrete complement each other to convey this defining narrative of the city’s history, with the message that the city and its inhabitants have always been able to recover and find new strength after adversity.

![_expand](visual_memory_of_the_fires_of_debrecen/14.webp)

In the interior of the glass installation, time is divided vertically, while the city is sliced horizontally. The size of the rings on the glass panels varies according to the number of burnt houses, making the repetition of historical events transparent and highlighting the major fires that destroyed the city.

![_full](visual_memory_of_the_fires_of_debrecen/4.webp)
![_full](visual_memory_of_the_fires_of_debrecen/9.webp)

The smallest triangle on the side is layered with maps from a study by Dr. Jenő Roncsik, showing the parts of the city that burnt down. In the middle triangle is the story of Mrs Szabó Gergelyné, in which the original 1681 witch trials are presented, where her husband seeks revenge for his convicted and executed wife. He threatened the town that as his wife would die in flames, so would Debrecen. This love that transcended time and space set the city aflame. Many houses and streets were burnt down, and Gergely Szabó was sentenced to death by hanging for arson. And the largest triangle has a montage of original archival handwritten documentary traces of the biggest fires in Debrecen in 1802 and 1811.

![_full](visual_memory_of_the_fires_of_debrecen/8.webp)
